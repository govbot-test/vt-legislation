Act No. M-9 (H.956). An act relating to approval of an amendment to the charter of the
City of Burlington relating to the Office of Racial Equity, Inclusion, and Belonging
Subjects: Municipal charters; amendment; municipal and county government; City
of Burlington
This act amends the charter of the City of Burlington to create the Office of Racial
Equity, Inclusion, and Belonging and to establish the permanent position of Director of
Racial Equity, Inclusion, and Belonging.
Effective Date: May 26, 2026.
