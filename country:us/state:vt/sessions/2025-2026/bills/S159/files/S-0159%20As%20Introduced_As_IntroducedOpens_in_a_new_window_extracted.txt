1 S.159
2 Introduced by Senator Cummings
3 Referred to Committee on
4 Date:
5 Subject: Public service; telecommunications; siting
6 Statement of purpose of bill as introduced: This bill proposes to remove the
7 sunset on the Public Utility Commission’s authority to issue certificates of
8 public good for telecommunications facilities.
9 An act relating to removing the sunset on 30 V.S.A. § 248a
10 It is hereby enacted by the General Assembly of the State of Vermont:
11 Sec. 1. 30 V.S.A. § 248a is amended to read:
12 § 248a. CERTIFICATE OF PUBLIC GOOD FOR COMMUNICATIONS
13 FACILITIES
14 * * *
15 (i) Sunset of Commission authority. Effective on July 1, 2026, no new
16 applications for certificates of public good under this section may be
17 considered by the Commission. [Repealed.]
18 * * *
19 Sec. 2. EFFECTIVE DATE
20 This act shall take effect on July 1, 2026.
VT LEG #385464 v.1
