Whereas, fornearly40years, MaryHoughtonhasenvisioned, initiated, andpromotedaffordablehousinginVermont’s

offinanceatthestill-experimentalandfledglingBurlingtonCommunityLandTrust(BCLT), withitsmissiontofacilitate
permanentlyaffordablehousingforbothrentingandownershipthroughcommunityownershipoftheunderlying
land, and
Whereas, MaryHoughton’sleadershipatBCLThasbeencharacterizedas“foundationaltotheorganization’searly
growthandenduringsuccess,”and“transformingitintooneofthemostrespectedcommunitylandtrustsinthecountry,”
and, in2002, theFordFoundationawardedtheorganizationanditsleaders, Houghtonandherco-director, BrendaTorpy,

exemplifiedherdedicationandpassionforthemissionofBCLT, andresultedinherdesignationasaco-director, and

DevelopmentCorporationintothelargerandgreater-resourcedChamplainHousingTrust, and
Whereas, MaryHoughtonbroughtheraffordablehousingexpertisetosouthernVermont, wherefornineyearsshe

Whereas, morerecently, asavolunteer, shehasservedontheboardsoftheWindham & WindsorHousingTrust,

Whereas, in2019, theVermontHousingandConservationBoardpresentedMaryHoughtonwithitsGeorgeNutting
CommunityCatalystAward, and
Whereas, asidefrom herexemplaryaffordablehousingefforts, MaryHoughtonhasservedontheboardofthePublic
SafetyProject, amunicipalcommunityimprovementeffortintheOldNorthEndofBurlington, and, since2019, onthe
LegislativeApportionmentBoard, now thereforebeit
ResolvedbytheSenateandHouseofRepresentatives:
ThattheGeneralAssemblyhonorsMaryHoughtonofBrattleboroasanextraordinaryadvocateforsustainableand
affordablehousing, andbeit further
Resolved: ThattheSecretaryofStatebedirectedtosendacopyofthisresolutiontoMaryHoughton.
