Section 1:
State of Vermont
Senate Chamber
Montpelier, Vermont
Senate Concurrent Resolution
Offered by All Members of the House,
S.C.R. 12. Senate concurrent resolution in memory of former Washington County Senator Mary
Just Skinner of Middlesex.
Whereas, a Hoosier by birth, Mary Just grew up in the Chicago area and graduated from Barnard
College and Columbia Law School in New York City, where she met her future husband and fellow
attorney, Scott Skinner; they married in 1970 and, in 1977, settled in Middlesex, where they raised two
sons and managed a beef cattle operation, and
Whereas, before hanging her professional shingle in Montpelier, Mary Just Skinner was employed
at Vermont Legal Aid, successfully advocating before the U.S. Court of Appeals for the Second Circuit
for the overturning of a U.S. Marine Corps policy automatically discharging pregnant Marines, and at
the Vermont Supreme Court, where she won a case requiring utilities to secure regulatory approval
before raising rates, and
Whereas, in 1978, at 32 years of age, Mary Just Skinner stood successfully for the first of seven
consecutive terms as a Vermont State Senator representing Washington County, chaired the Finance
and Judiciary committees, and served as assistant minority leader, and
Whereas, as a State Senator, she was especially focused on the challenges vulnerable Vermonters
encountered and on protecting the environment, and
Whereas, after leaving the Senate, Mary Just Skinner turned her civic attention to local education
governance, serving for 24 years on the Middlesex School Board, and, most recently, she was a
member of the Human Services Board and was on the board of directors of Washington Electric
Cooperative, and
Whereas, she was actively engaged in many community organizations; enjoyed collecting folk art;
was a voracious reader; and, in midlife, became an active athlete who loved to hike, including in the
Himalayas, and, at 59 years of age, she summited Mt. Kilimanjaro in Africa, and
Whereas, Mary Just Skinner, whose husband passed in 2018, died unexpectedly on January 9, 2026,
at 79 years of age, while visiting her children and grandchildren in California, and she served
Vermonters with dedication and enthusiasm, now therefore be it
Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives:
That the General Assembly expresses its sincere condolences to the family of former Washington
County Senator Mary Just Skinner of Middlesex, and be it further
Resolved: That the Secretary of State be directed to send a copy of this resolution to the family of
Mary Just Skinner.
