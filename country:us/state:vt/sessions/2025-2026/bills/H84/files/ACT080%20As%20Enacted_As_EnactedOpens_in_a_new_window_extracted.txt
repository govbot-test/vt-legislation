Section 2:
§ 9361. HEALTH CARE PROVIDERS DELIVERING HEALTH CARE
SERVICES THROUGH TELEMEDICINE OR BY STORE-AND-

* * *
(d) Neither a health care provider nor a patient shall create or cause to be

unless both the patient and the provider consent to the recording.
* * *

Section 3:
§ 9362. HEALTH CARE PROVIDERS DELIVERING HEALTH CARE

(d) Neither a health care provider nor a patient shall create or cause to be
