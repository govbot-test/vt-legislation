Section 1:
State of Vermont
House of Representatives
Montpelier, Vermont
Concurrent House Resolution
H.C.R. 155
House concurrent resolution honoring Melvin and Susan Coburn for their nearly half-century of superb
proprietorship of Coburns’ General Store in South Strafford and congratulating the Strafford Community Trust
on a successful fundraising campaign to preserve this historic mercantile establishment
Whereas, since the 1880s, South Strafford has been the home of a beloved general store that, for nearly half
a century, featured the dedicated proprietorship of Melvin and Susan Coburn, including 27 years when their
daughter, Chrissy Jamieson, worked beside her parents for 12-hour days, and
Whereas, the Coburns operated a truly full-service general store, where not only grocery and other in-
demand products were sold, but they also hosted a bank, a deer weighing station, a gas pump, a tiny
laundromat, and the local U.S. Post Office, and the store also welcomed local nonprofit organizations’ bake
sales and maintained a lost and found drop-off service, and
Whereas, typical of their hometown relationships, the Coburns extended exceptional kindness to local
youngsters, including the then-young and now-popular music star Noah Kahan, but although the Coburns
extended a warm welcome to his fans who traveled to South Strafford, the couple faithfully protected the
Kahan family’s privacy, never revealing a home address, and
Whereas, after decades of ensuring their store was fully stocked and ready to serve customers from near and
far, the Coburns desired to step back from their daily duties and sell to a member of the younger
generation, and
Whereas, this objective proved challenging, and friends of Coburns’ General Store, both in Strafford and
nearby Upper Valley communities, spearheaded a successful $1.8 million fundraising campaign to purchase the
store under the rubric of the Strafford Community Trust, and
Whereas, this nonprofit model of general store ownership, which has proven successful in other Vermont
communities, is now, with the guidance of new management, ensuring the continuance and vitality of the store,
now therefore be it
Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives:
That the General Assembly honors Melvin and Susan Coburn for their nearly half-century of superb
proprietorship of Coburns’ General Store in South Strafford and congratulates the Strafford Community Trust
on a successful fundraising campaign to preserve this historic mercantile establishment, and be it further
Resolved: That the Secretary of State be directed to send a copy of this resolution to the Coburn family, the
Strafford Community Trust, and the store.
