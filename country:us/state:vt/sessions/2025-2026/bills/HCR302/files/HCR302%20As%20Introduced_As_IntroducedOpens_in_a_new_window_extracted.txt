Whereas, ArtandKathyWhitmanarelifelongresidentsoftheShaftsburyarea, andduringtheir60-yearmarriage, the

generousdonorstolocalanimalrescueorganizations, and
Whereas, astheownersofA&KAgriservices, theWhitmanssponsorlocalcommunityevents, includingtheGarlic
TownUSAFestival, and
Whereas, intheirrolesasaward-winningNorShaftLions, thecouplehassupportedeffortsaidingdisabledindividuals,
ahealthyenvironment, andseniors;theWhitmanshavechairedvariouscommunityfunctions, includinganannualEaster
egghunt, acharitygolftournament, achilifest, Christmastreesales, theNightforSightDinnerDance, Oktoberfest, the
PeacePosterContest, andyouthcamps;theyarealsolong-term supportersoftheShaftsburyHistoricalSocietyandthe
EagleSquareMuseum, and, inDecember2025, theyorganizedthetown’sfirsttractorparade, and
Whereas, aspresidentandsecretary-treasurer, respectively, oftheBenningtonCountyFarm Bureau, ArtandKathy
Whitmanadvocateforforward-lookingagriculturalpolicythatisinthefarmingcommunity’sbestinterests, and
Whereas, ArtWhitmanhasservedhistownandStateinelectedandappointedpositions;asamemberofthe
ShaftsburySelectboard(2014–2024), includingaschairofthetown’seconomicdevelopmentandwatercommittees, he
advocatedforthecreationoftheShaftsburyTownGreenandcommunitycenter;andheservedasamemberofthe
VermontRailAdvisoryCouncil, and

aretherecipientsofthetown’s2026OrdinaryHeroAward, anhonorthatisrichlydeserved, now therefore be it
