State of Vermont
House of Representatives
Montpelier, Vermont
Concurrent House Resolution
H.C.R. 296
Bennington, Durfee of Shaftsbury, and Nigro of Bennington
Offered by Senators Bongartz and Plunkett
House concurrent resolution congratulating Inspirational Bible Book & Gifts of Bennington, Vermont’s last
remaining Christian bookstore, on its 50th anniversary
Whereas, religion has always played a central role in the life of Jacob Esh, who was born in 1941 and raised
in Lancaster County, Pennsylvania, in a traditional Amish farming family, and
Whereas, in 1970, he married Lorraine, with whom he has happily shared his life for the past 55 years, and
they began selling Christian books from store racks located in bus stations, grocery stores, pharmacies, and
other locations, which eventually surpassed 40 points of sale, and
Whereas, in December 1975, Jacob and Lorraine Esh decided to consolidate their retail business to a single
site and rented a small space inside a mall on North Street in Bennington, and
Whereas, after several moves, in 1982, Jacob and Lorraine Esh purchased a duplex at 110 County Street that
would serve as their residence until 1995 and the store’s home ever since, and
Whereas, sales were steady during the 1980s, when roughly 30 Christian bookstores operated in Vermont,
and
Whereas, in the store’s early years, approximately one-third of the revenue was derived from musical-related
sales, including sheet music and CDs, and
Whereas, in the digital retail age, the market for religious-related merchandise, such as greeting cards and
music products, is no longer robust, and Inspirational Bible Book & Gifts is today the last traditional Christian
bookstore operating in Vermont, and
Whereas, despite this economic challenge, Jacob Esh proudly opens the store each business day, a mission
based on his abiding religious faith, now therefore be it
Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives:
That the General Assembly congratulates Inspirational Bible Book & Gifts of Bennington, Vermont’s last
remaining Christian bookstore, on its 50th anniversary, and be it further
Resolved: That the Secretary of State be directed to send a copy of this resolution to Inspirational Bible
Book & Gifts.
